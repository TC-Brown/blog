Authentic Rigatoni alla Carbonara

by @MarcoPancetta • ⭐ 4.9 (230 reviews) • ⏱️ 20 mins

No cream allowed. A traditional Roman pasta dish made using only egg yolks, crispy guanciale, Pecorino Romano, and black pepper.

Prep Time
5m
Cook Time
15m
Servings
3
Difficulty
Medium

🍳 Ingredients

📝 Instructions

1

Cook Guanciale

Add cubed guanciale to a cold skillet. Bring to medium heat and cook until crispy and the fat renders. Remove skillet from heat, keep guanciale in fat.

2

Make Egg & Cheese Mixture

Whisk egg yolks, whole egg, grated Pecorino Romano, and a generous amount of black pepper together in a bowl until a thick paste forms.

3

Cook Pasta

Boil rigatoni in salted water until just al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.

4

Combine & Emulsify

Add drained pasta directly to the skillet with guanciale and fat, tossing for a minute to coat. Let it cool slightly, then pour in the egg-cheese paste. Toss quickly while adding splashes of reserved pasta water to create a creamy, emulsified sauce. Serve immediately topped with extra Pecorino and black pepper.
