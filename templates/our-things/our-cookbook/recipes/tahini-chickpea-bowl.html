Tahini Chickpea & Roasted Veggie Bowl

by @FitFoodie • ⭐ 4.7 (31 reviews) • ⏱️ 30 mins

Nourishing grain bowl topped with turmeric roasted cauliflower, kale, protein-packed chickpeas, quinoa, and a creamy garlic tahini dressing.

Prep Time
10m
Cook Time
20m
Servings
2
Difficulty
Easy

🍳 Ingredients

📝 Instructions

1

Roast Veggies & Chickpeas

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss cauliflower florets and chickpeas with olive oil, turmeric, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20 minutes until tender and slightly golden.

2

Make Tahini Dressing

In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, minced garlic, lemon juice, salt, and a splash of warm water. Add more water as needed until a smooth, pourable dressing forms.

3

Massage Kale

Drizzle kale with a tiny bit of olive oil and a pinch of salt, then massage with your hands for 1-2 minutes until soft and dark green.

4

Assemble Bowl

Divide cooked quinoa, massaged kale, roasted cauliflower, and crispy chickpeas into serving bowls. Drizzle generously with garlic tahini dressing and serve warm.
