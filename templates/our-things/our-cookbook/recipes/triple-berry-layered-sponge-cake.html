Triple Berry Layered Sponge Cake

by @BakeWithChloe • ⭐ 5.0 (112 reviews) • ⏱️ 50 mins

Fluffy vanilla sponge filled with fresh raspberries, blueberries, and whipped cream. Perfectly light, sweet, and ideal for Sunday baking.

Prep Time
20m
Cook Time
30m
Servings
8
Difficulty
Medium

🍳 Ingredients

📝 Instructions

1

Preheat & Prep

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.

2

Mix Dry Ingredients

Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.

3

Add Butter & Liquids

Beat in butter, milk, and vanilla extract until smooth, then gently fold in beaten egg whites.

4

Bake

Divide batter evenly between the prepared pans. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

5

Cool & Assemble

Allow cake layers to cool completely. Whip the heavy cream to stiff peaks. Spread a thick layer of whipped cream and half of the fresh berries on top of the first cake layer. Top with the second cake layer, and decorate with the remaining cream and berries.
