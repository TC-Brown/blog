☀️ Growing Tips

LIGHT: Bright, indirect light to low light tolerances.

WATER: Allow the top half of soil to dry between waterings.

SOIL: Standard well-draining potting soil mix.

🌱 Sowing / Propagation

METHOD: Easiest from stem cuttings rooted in water or moist soil.

NODE: Ensure cutting has at least one node below the water line.

ROOTING: Roots typically appear within 2–3 weeks.
